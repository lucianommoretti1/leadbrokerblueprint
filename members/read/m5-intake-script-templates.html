How to use these scripts

Phone-based intake script templates for four representative verticals. They are starting points, not finished scripts; adapt them to your specific buyer's qualification specifications, your state, and your operational policies.

Each template includes: opening, qualification questions in suggested order, confirmation flow, disqualification close, and notes on vertical-specific compliance considerations. Use the templates in combination with Module 5 (intake and qualification design) and Module 3 (regulatory framework).

Vertical 1 — Personal Injury (PI)

Phone-based intake. Goal: confirm qualification and schedule the attorney consult or transfer the call. Average call: 5-10 minutes.

Opening

Hi [First Name], this is [Agent Name] with [Firm Name]. I'm following up on the case-evaluation request you submitted at [time]. Do you have 5-10 minutes to talk about what happened?

Qualification questions (in order)

  1. When did the accident occur? [DISQUALIFY if outside statute of limitations for the state]
  2. Were you injured? [DISQUALIFY if "no"]
  3. Did you receive or are you currently receiving medical treatment? [WEIGHT — treatment is a key qualifier]
  4. Have you already retained an attorney for this matter? [DISQUALIFY if "yes"]
  5. Were you at fault, or was the other party? [WEIGHT — at-fault is generally a disqualifier]
  6. What state did the accident occur in? [Confirm jurisdiction]
  7. Where are you located today?
  8. On a scale of 1 to 10, how severe were your injuries? [INFORMATIONAL]

Confirmation and next step

  • Confirm: full name, best phone number, email address, mailing address.
  • Explain next step: "Our intake team or one of our attorneys will call you back within [X hours] during business hours to discuss your case in more detail. The call is free and there is no obligation."
  • If transferring live: "I'm going to connect you now with [attorney/intake specialist] who handles cases like yours — please hold one moment."

Disqualified close

  • Polite close: "Based on what you've told me, this isn't a case our firm typically handles. I want to be respectful of your time and not waste it on a process that wouldn't move forward. I'd recommend [bar referral service / public legal aid resource if appropriate]. Best of luck with your recovery."
  • Do not promise the firm will reconsider; do not transfer to a different vertical.

Vertical-specific notes

Document responses in your intake CRM with timestamps. Save the TrustedForm certificate URL with the lead record. If the consumer asks to revoke consent or stop being contacted, honor it immediately and propagate within 10 business days per the FCC revocation rule.

Vertical 2 — HVAC (replacement / new system)

Phone-based intake for exclusive leads; form-only for shared. Goal: confirm homeowner status, system age, timeline; schedule the in-home estimate or transfer to the contractor. Average call: 3-6 minutes.

Opening

Hi [First Name], this is [Agent Name] following up on your HVAC quote request from [date/time]. Got a few quick questions to make sure we connect you with the right contractor.

Qualification questions (in order)

  1. Are you the homeowner, or do you rent? [DISQUALIFY if renter]
  2. What type of system are you looking at — central air, heat pump, ductless mini-split, full replacement, or just service? [ROUTE based on contractor specialty]
  3. How old is your current system? [WEIGHT — 10+ years is a stronger replacement lead]
  4. What's your timeline — within 30 days, 30-90 days, or longer? [WEIGHT — within 90 days is the strong tier]
  5. What's your zip code or address? [Confirm service area]
  6. Are you currently working with another contractor on this project? [INFORMATIONAL — affects exclusivity claim]
  7. Approximate square footage of your home? [INFORMATIONAL for sizing]

Confirmation and next step

  • Confirm: full name, best phone number, email, service address.
  • Explain next step: "A licensed [HVAC contractor name] technician will call you within the next [hour during business hours] to schedule a free in-home estimate. The estimate is at no cost to you and there's no obligation to buy."
  • If transferring live: "I'm going to connect you with [contractor name] now — please hold."

Disqualified close

  • Renter close: "Since you're renting, you'd want to coordinate with your landlord or property management before scheduling a contractor. I'd recommend reaching out to them first. Thanks for considering us."
  • Out-of-area close: "Unfortunately our partner contractors don't cover your area. Best of luck with your project."

Vertical-specific notes

If the consumer reports an active emergency (no heat in winter, no AC during heatwave, water leak), prioritize the call to the contractor immediately and flag it as emergency in the CRM. Emergencies convert at higher rates than standard replacement leads.

Vertical 3 — Medicare (AEP and SEP)

Phone-based intake. The vertical is heavily regulated: CMS scope-of-appointment requirements, recording rules, and prohibited representations apply. Average call: 5-8 minutes.

Opening

Hi [First Name], this is [Agent Name] calling on a recorded line. [Pause for recording disclosure.] I'm following up on the Medicare plan-comparison request you submitted at [time]. Is now a good time to talk about your Medicare options?

CMS-required disclosures (Medicare-specific)

This call is being recorded for quality assurance.

We are not affiliated with or endorsed by the Medicare program, the U.S. Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services, or any federal government agency.

We represent [N] organizations which offer [Y] products in your service area. The products represented are [types — e.g., Medicare Advantage, Medicare Supplement, Part D].

Please confirm: am I speaking with [First Name Last Name]?

Qualification questions (in order)

  1. Are you currently enrolled in original Medicare, Medicare Advantage, or neither? [ROUTE]
  2. What is your zip code? [Confirm service area]
  3. Are you within an applicable election period? [CONFIRM: AEP Oct 15-Dec 7, IEP around 65th birthday, SEP for qualifying life events]
  4. Are you currently working with another agent or broker on your Medicare options? [INFORMATIONAL — affects scope of conversation]
  5. Would you like to discuss your options today, or would you prefer to set an appointment for a more detailed conversation?

Scope of Appointment (Medicare-specific)

If the conversation will move into specific plan recommendations or enrollment, capture the CMS-required Scope of Appointment authorization BEFORE making any plan-specific recommendations. The SOA records what types of products will be discussed. Without SOA, no plan-specific discussion.

Confirmation and next step

  • Confirm: full name, phone, address, date of birth (for verification only; for actual enrollment, full DOB and Medicare number are needed but only after SOA is captured).
  • Set next step: scheduled appointment with licensed agent OR live transfer to licensed agent if transferring now.

Disqualified close

  • Wrong election period: "It looks like you're outside an applicable Medicare election period right now. The next Annual Election Period runs October 15 through December 7. I'll make a note to follow up with you in [month before AEP]. Thanks for your time."
  • Already working with an agent: "It sounds like you've got someone helping you. We don't want to step on toes — best to continue with your current agent. Thanks for your time."

Vertical-specific notes

Medicare is heavily regulated. Every conversation must follow CMS marketing rules: no use of Medicare logos or names suggesting government endorsement; no statements that the consumer must enroll today; no claims about plan benefits not supported by the specific plan documents; SOA before plan-specific discussion. Recording retention typically 10 years per CMS guidance.

Vertical 4 — Mortgage purchase

Phone-based intake for higher-value leads; form-only for top-of-funnel. NMLS licensing applies for the lender side. Goal: confirm intent, timeline, and pre-qualification status; transfer to loan officer. Average call: 5-10 minutes.

Opening

Hi [First Name], this is [Agent Name] following up on the home loan inquiry you submitted at [time]. Do you have a few minutes to talk about your home purchase plans?

Qualification questions (in order)

  1. Are you looking to purchase a primary residence, a second home, or an investment property? [ROUTE]
  2. What's your timeline — within 30 days, 30-90 days, 90-180 days, or longer? [WEIGHT — short timeline is the strong tier]
  3. Have you been pre-approved for a mortgage, or are you in the research phase? [WEIGHT — pre-approved consumers convert higher]
  4. What's your approximate credit score range — 740+, 680-739, 620-679, or below 620?
  5. What's your approximate household income range?
  6. Have you identified a property, or are you still searching?
  7. What state and city are you looking to buy in? [Confirm lender service area]
  8. Are you a first-time buyer, or have you purchased before?

Confirmation and next step

  • Confirm: full name, phone, email, current address (for licensing/jurisdictional purposes only — no SSN, no full DOB, no income verification on this call).
  • Set next step: "A licensed loan officer will call you within [time window] to discuss your options in more detail. They'll be able to give you a sense of available rates and programs. No obligation; no commitment until you formally apply."
  • If transferring live: "Connecting you now to [loan officer name], NMLS ID [number]. One moment."

Disqualified close

  • Far-out timeline: "Since you're more than six months out, the rate environment will likely shift before you're ready to lock. I'd recommend reaching back out closer to your purchase window so we can give you accurate numbers. Mind if I follow up with you in [N months]?"
  • Out-of-state for lender: "Our partner lenders don't currently offer loans in [state]. Best of luck with your home search."

Vertical-specific notes

Mortgage marketing must comply with RESPA Section 8 (no kickbacks or unearned fees), NMLS state licensing rules, and consumer-finance UDAAP standards. Loan officers contacting consumers must identify themselves with their NMLS ID. Do not make representations about specific rates, approval likelihood, or program eligibility on this intake call — those are the licensed loan officer's job after the formal application.

Cross-vertical principles

  1. Capture every call's outcome in the CRM with timestamps. Document the qualifying answers; the documentation supports your reps to buyers and your defense against disputes.
  2. Save the TrustedForm or Jornaya consent-certificate URL on every lead record, every time.
  3. Honor STOP, opt-out, or revocation requests immediately. Propagate revocations through your distribution platform within 10 business days, per the April 2025 FCC revocation rule (Module 3.3).
  4. Use a consistent script structure across agents in a vertical — opening, qualification, confirmation, close. Variance reduces conversion.
  5. Quality-monitor a sample of calls per week. Review opening rate, qualification adherence, and close strength. Use the monitoring to coach and refine.
  6. Do not improvise vertical-regulatory compliance. CMS rules for Medicare, RESPA Section 8 for mortgage, ABA Rules for legal, EKRA for addiction treatment — each is a distinct regime; agents need vertical-specific training, not general intake training.

Standing disclosures

These templates are research-based starting points, not finished operational scripts. Vertical-specific regulations apply and counsel familiar with your vertical should review your finalized scripts. The course author is a curriculum builder, not a multi-million-dollar lead-gen operator.
