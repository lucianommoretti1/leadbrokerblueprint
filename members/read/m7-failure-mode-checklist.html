Failure-Mode Checklist

Module 7 deliverable

← Module 7 overview Download PDF

Six failure modes and how to defend against each — review monthly

How to use this checklist

Six failure modes from Module 7.6 plus targeted defenses for each. Review monthly. Mark items "OK" if your operation has working defenses in place; "At risk" if you don't.

Most operator post-mortems trace back to two or three of these modes. Catching them early — while they're still cheap to address — is the entire point of the monthly review.

Failure mode 1 — Buyer churn

Buyer relationship ends within 30-90 days; lead quality doesn't match what the buyer's intake operation needs.

Defense checklist (mark ☐ OK or ⚠ At risk)

☐ OK ⚠ At risk LPA qualification specifications are precise (not vague terms like "qualified" without definition).

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Lead delivery is matched to specifications; weekly QA sample confirms match.

☐ OK ⚠ At risk I have a weekly check-in or report cadence with the buyer; surprises are caught early.

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Dispute rate by buyer is tracked and stays within industry-normal ranges (5-20% by lead type).

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Buyer-side feedback is collected and applied: at least one specification adjustment per quarter based on buyer input.

Failure mode 2 — Traffic-cost inflation

CPCs or CPMs rise through the campaign's life; thin starting margins are squeezed.

Defense checklist (mark ☐ OK or ⚠ At risk)

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Starting margins are 30%+ gross; if 15-25%, watch closely; if under 15%, restructure or stop.

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Multiple traffic sources running (don't be dependent on one platform).

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Negative-keyword and exclusion-audience audits quarterly.

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Creative refresh cadence (paid social) per platform's typical fatigue window.

☐ OK ⚠ At risk If LSA is active in vertical, my value proposition extends beyond "we get you leads."

Failure mode 3 — Regulatory shifts

A regulatory change (FCC, FTC, state) shifts what's permissible or required; operators who didn't track regulation get caught.

Defense checklist (mark ☐ OK or ⚠ At risk)

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Quarterly compliance review completed (Module 3 deliverable).

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Subscribed to at least 2 law-firm-published TCPA / FTC update feeds (Klein Moynihan Turco, Eversheds Sutherland, etc.).

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Consent disclosures audited within last 90 days.

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Vendor compliance (TrustedForm, Jornaya configurations) reviewed within last 90 days.

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Counsel relationship established or on-call for vertical-specific questions.

Failure mode 4 — Exclusivity demands

Growing buyer requires exclusivity or wider exclusivity than the seller can sustain inventory-wise.

Defense checklist (mark ☐ OK or ⚠ At risk)

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Inventory volume is monitored against current and contracted demand; headroom > 20% maintained.

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Multiple buyers per vertical (not 60%+ concentration on a single buyer).

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Pricing strategy distinguishes shared from exclusive; able to flex between them.

☐ OK ⚠ At risk If a key buyer asks for tighter exclusivity, I have a written analysis of supply/demand impact before agreeing.

Failure mode 5 — Payment-terms breakdown

Buyer extends payment timing (net 30 → 60 → 90) or disputes invoices aggressively; cash flow breaks.

Defense checklist (mark ☐ OK or ⚠ At risk)

☐ OK ⚠ At risk DSO (days sales outstanding) tracked monthly; trends are visible.

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Working-capital reserve at 30-60 days of operating costs maintained.

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Late-payment policy and interest in every LPA.

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Buyer concentration: no single buyer > 40% of revenue (target).

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Credit-risk diligence on new mid-size and large buyers before signing (D&B / public filings).

Failure mode 6 — Founder burnout

Solo founder runs sales, intake, ops, and compliance personally for too long; quality degrades; relationships drift.

Defense checklist (mark ☐ OK or ⚠ At risk)

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Weekly tracking of hours actually worked vs. planned.

☐ OK ⚠ At risk First VA / intake hire planned for when monthly revenue clears [target] (Module 6.5).

☐ OK ⚠ At risk At least one hour per week scheduled for strategy / not tactical work.

☐ OK ⚠ At risk Vacation taken in the last 90 days, with a defined coverage plan.

☐ OK ⚠ At risk If I dread three or more recurring tasks in the operation, I'm building a path to delegate them.
