Notes on citation format

Federal statutes are cited by U.S. Code section (e.g., 47 U.S.C. § 227).

Federal regulations are cited by Code of Federal Regulations section (e.g., 47 C.F.R. § 64.1200).

Federal court opinions are cited by case name, reporter (where published), and court / year (e.g., 592 U.S. 395 (2021)). Recent unpublished or slip opinions use docket number and date (e.g., No. 24-10277 (11th Cir. Jan. 24, 2025)).

State statutes are cited by state name and statute number; format varies by state.

Agency bulletins and press releases are cited by agency, document title, and date.

Standing disclosures

This is a research-based reference, not legal advice. Citations are current as of May 2026 and will be re-researched on a quarterly cadence. Verify each citation against the primary source before relying on it.
