Every regulatory and operational acronym used across Modules 1–3.

How to use this glossary

Distinct from Module 1's full glossary, which defines every industry term. This document is acronyms only — for fast recognition when you encounter them in a regulation, a buyer contract, or a trade conversation.

Sorted alphabetically. Three columns: acronym, what it stands for, and one line explaining what it actually means in the lead brokering context.

Acronyms A – Z

Acronym

Stands for

In one line

ABA

American Bar Association

Source of Model Rules of Professional Conduct.

ACA

Affordable Care Act

Marketplace health insurance; AEP/OEP rules apply.

AEP

Annual Election Period

Medicare enrollment window, Oct. 15 – Dec. 7.

AKS

Anti-Kickback Statute

42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7b; federal healthcare-program kickbacks.

ATDS

Automatic Telephone Dialing System

TCPA-defined equipment; narrowed by Duguid (2021).

BBB

Better Business Bureau

Self-regulatory ad review and consumer complaints.

CAC

Customer Acquisition Cost

CPL × leads per closed customer.

CAN-SPAM

Controlling the Assault of Non-Solicited Pornography and Marketing

Commercial email regulation; 15 U.S.C. § 7701.

CCPA

California Consumer Privacy Act

First U.S. comprehensive state privacy law; CPRA expanded it.

CFPB

Consumer Financial Protection Bureau

Federal financial services regulator; UDAAP / RESPA enforcement.

CFR

Code of Federal Regulations

Where federal agency rules are codified.

CMS

Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services

Federal agency; Medicare marketing rules.

CPA

Cost Per Acquisition

Per-converted-customer cost; also a pricing model.

CPC

Cost Per Click

Paid-search and paid-social ad pricing unit.

CPL

Cost Per Lead

Per-lead price; supply CPL vs buyer-side CPL.

CPM

Cost Per Mille (thousand impressions)

Display and social ad pricing unit.

CPRA

California Privacy Rights Act

CCPA amendment; established CPPA; effective 2023.

CSBS

Conference of State Bank Supervisors

Administers NMLS.

DNC

Do Not Call (Registry)

National Do Not Call Registry under TCPA/TSR.

DOJ

Department of Justice

Federal prosecutor; enforces EKRA and AKS criminally.

E-SIGN

Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act

Recognizes electronic signatures (15 U.S.C. § 7001).

EBR

Existing Business Relationship

TCPA / DNC exemption under specific conditions.

EKRA

Eliminating Kickbacks in Recovery Act

18 U.S.C. § 220; 2018; addiction-treatment marketing.

FCC

Federal Communications Commission

TCPA rulemaking and DNC.

FCRA

Fair Credit Reporting Act

15 U.S.C. § 1681; consumer-report regulation.

FTC

Federal Trade Commission

Advertising / reviews / TSR / endorsement enforcement.

GPC

Global Privacy Control

Browser-based opt-out signal honored under several state laws.

HIPAA

Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act

PHI privacy and security; 45 C.F.R. Parts 160, 164.

IMC

Insurance Marketing Coalition

Petitioner in IMC v. FCC (2025); industry trade group.

IMO/FMO

Insurance / Field Marketing Organization

Aggregates insurance agent demand and supply.

IVR

Interactive Voice Response

Automated phone menu; common in pay-per-call.

LTV

Lifetime Value

Total gross profit from a customer.

MDL

Multidistrict Litigation

Federal procedure for mass tort cases.

MQL

Marketing-Qualified Lead

B2B lead that has met marketing-side qualification criteria.

NAIC

National Association of Insurance Commissioners

Model laws for state insurance regulators.

NMLS

Nationwide Multistate Licensing System

Mortgage licensing platform; administered by CSBS.

OCR

Office for Civil Rights (HHS)

Enforces HIPAA.

PCI

Payment Card Industry (data security standards)

Card-data security; not a federal regulation but compliance-relevant.

PEWC

Prior Express Written Consent

TCPA standard for autodialed/prerecorded marketing.

PHI

Protected Health Information

HIPAA-protected health data.

PI

Personal Injury / Private Investigator

Vertical-dependent; both appear in this course.

RESPA

Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act

12 U.S.C. § 2601; mortgage anti-kickback (Section 8).

SQM/SQL

Sales-Qualified Meeting / Lead

B2B deliverable beyond MQL.

SUPPORT

Substance Use-Disorder Prevention…

2018 Act that included EKRA.

TCPA

Telephone Consumer Protection Act

47 U.S.C. § 227; the dominant calling-rules statute.

TDPSA

Texas Data Privacy and Security Act

Texas comprehensive privacy law (2024).

TSR

Telemarketing Sales Rule

16 C.F.R. Part 310; FTC telemarketing rule.

UCPA

Utah Consumer Privacy Act

Utah comprehensive privacy law (2023).

UDAAP

Unfair, Deceptive, or Abusive Acts or Practices

CFPB authority; 12 U.S.C. § 5531.

UDAP

Unfair and Deceptive Acts and Practices

State-level unfair-practices statutes (analogous to FTC § 5).

VCDPA

Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act

Virginia comprehensive privacy law (2023).

Acronyms by category

Federal regulators

FCC, FTC, CFPB, CMS, DOJ, OCR (HHS).

Federal statutes

TCPA, CAN-SPAM, FCRA, RESPA, AKS, EKRA, E-SIGN.

FTC / CFPB rules

TSR, FTC Endorsement Guides, FTC Reviews Rule, UDAAP, UDAP.

Privacy regimes

CCPA, CPRA, VCDPA, CPA, CTDPA, UCPA, TDPSA, GPC.

Healthcare-specific

HIPAA, PHI, AKS, EKRA, OCR.

Operational terms

CAC, LTV, CPL, CPC, CPM, CPA (cost per acquisition), MQL, SQL, SQM, IVR.

Lead-gen-specific

PEWC, ATDS, DNC, EBR, IMC (Insurance Marketing Coalition), IMO/FMO, AEP.

Standing disclosures

This is a research-based reference. Acronyms and their underlying statutes / rules are current as of May 2026; verify against primary sources before relying on any specific claim about scope or applicability.
