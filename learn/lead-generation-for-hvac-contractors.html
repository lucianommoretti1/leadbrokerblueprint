Lead generation for HVAC contractors — what works in 2026

How HVAC lead generation actually works in 2026 — who buys, what they pay, which channels produce, and how to build an operation that survives competitive pressure.

The HVAC lead market in 2026

HVAC replacement is one of the largest and most consistent lead-gen verticals in U.S. service businesses. Demand is durable (every HVAC system fails eventually), regulatory compliance is manageable (much less burdensome than Medicare or PI), and the buyer pool is large and accessible to new operators.

Who buys HVAC leads

  • Independent local contractors (1-5 trucks). Owner-operated. Volume per buyer: 5-15 leads/mo.
  • Regional contractor chains (10-50 locations). Volume: 50-300 leads/mo.
  • PE-rollup home services companies (Wrench Group, ARS, others). Volume: 100-1,000+ leads/mo.
  • Manufacturer dealer networks (Carrier Authorized, Lennox Premier). Brand-affiliated with co-op marketing budgets.

Pricing as of 2026

  • Shared form leads: $35-$75 each.
  • Exclusive form leads: $75-$150 each.
  • Phone-qualified estimate appointments: $150-$350 each.
  • Live warm transfers (consumer on line, contractor accepts): $200-$400 each.

Channels that work

Google Search Ads (primary, 50-70% of typical volume), NextDoor (hyperlocal, homeowner-dense, lower CPC than Meta), direct mail to new-mover lists (high-trust, slower), and Meta paid lookalikes. Modern operators are also testing AI Search Optimization (getting cited in ChatGPT/Perplexity for "best HVAC in [city]"), and AI voice agents for first-touch intake.

Compliance gotchas

State contractor-license requirements (FL, CA, GA, NC, VA all require licensure — confirm your buyer holds one). TCPA on outbound calls to homeowners. Estimate-vs-quote disclosure to manage downstream disputes.

30-day starter action plan

  1. Pick 2-3 metros with adequate contractor density.
  2. Confirm 5+ contractor candidates per metro via the Module 4 outreach sequence.
  3. Set up Tier 1 stack (TrustedForm, Ringba/CallRail, landing page) + DNC scrubbing.
  4. Launch Google Ads at $30-$50/day per metro. Phone-qualify every lead within 5 minutes.
  5. Sign first pilot agreement within 21 days. Deliver at quality before scaling spend.
