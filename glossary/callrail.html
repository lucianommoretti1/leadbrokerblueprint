CallRail

A call tracking and attribution platform widely used by lead generators and service businesses.

CallRail is a call tracking and attribution platform. It provides tracked numbers, call recording, conversation-intelligence analysis, and attribution back to the ad campaign or content source that drove the call.

How operators use it

Two main use cases:

  • Form-and-call hybrid lead generators who need attribution across both intake types.
  • Service businesses themselves who want to know which marketing efforts drive calls.

CallRail is less specialized for high-volume pay-per-call routing than Ringba. For operations where the call is the primary product, Ringba is typically the right choice. For operations where calls are one of several lead types, CallRail's broader feature set wins.

Pricing

Plans start around $40-$50/mo and scale to several hundred monthly at high call volume.
