IVR (Interactive Voice Response)

Automated phone menus and qualification flows used in pay-per-call lead generation.

Interactive Voice Response (IVR) is the automated phone-menu technology that routes and pre-qualifies inbound callers — "Press 1 for HVAC service, press 2 for plumbing." In pay-per-call lead generation, IVR is the first-touch qualification layer that filters callers before connecting them to buyers.

How operators use IVR

  • Initial routing: vertical or sub-vertical disambiguation when one tracking number serves multiple campaigns.
  • Geographic filtering: confirming the caller is in the buyer's service area before connecting.
  • Basic qualification: simple yes/no questions that filter clearly disqualified callers.
  • Call duration triggers: meeting buyer minimum-call thresholds before billable status.
  • Recording disclosure: state-compliant notice that the call is being recorded.

Modern IVR vs AI voice agents

Traditional IVR uses pre-recorded prompts and DTMF (touch-tone) input. AI voice agents (Vapi, Bland.ai, Retell, Synthflow) replace IVR with conversational LLM-driven qualification — same job, but the caller can speak naturally instead of pressing buttons. As of 2026, AI voice agents are crossing the indistinguishable-from-human threshold and rapidly displacing traditional IVR for sophisticated operators.
